About kidney Health


,

Keeping your kidneys healthy is essential for overall well-being! Here are some key ways to maintain good kidney health:

  • Stay Hydrated: Drinking enough water helps your kidneys filter waste and toxins efficiently
  • Eat a Balanced Diet: Reduce salt intake, eat fresh fruits and vegetables, and avoid processed foods
  • Exercise Regularly: Staying active helps regulate blood pressure and reduces the risk of kidney disease
  • Monitor Blood Sugar & Blood Pressure: High blood sugar and hypertension can damage your kidneys over time.
  • Avoid Smoking & Excessive Alcohol: These habits can harm kidney function and increase health risks
  • Be Cautious with Medications: Overuse of painkillers and certain supplements can strain your kidneys.
