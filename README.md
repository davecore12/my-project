# Kidney Health Awareness Project

Welcome to the **Kidney Health Awareness Project**! This responsive website is designed to educate visitors about kidney physiology, chronic kidney disease (CKD), treatment modalities, renal nutrition, and community support initiatives.

## 🚀 Live Demo
You can explore the live website hosted on GitHub Pages:  
👉 **[https://davecore12.github.io/my-project/](https://davecore12.github.io/my-project/)**

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Educational Fundamentals:** In-depth breakdown of kidney anatomy, functions, filtration processes, and hormonal balance.
* **Global & Regional Statistics:** Up-to-date epidemiology, risk demographics, and health disparities (diabetes, hypertension, aging populations).
* **Myths vs. Facts:** Debunking common misunderstandings regarding kidney disease, silent symptoms, pain, and dialysis.
* **Treatment & Management:** Comprehensive guide to lifestyle modifications, renal pharmacotherapy, dialysis modalities (hemodialysis vs. peritoneal), and kidney transplantation.
* **Community Events & Search:** Filterable directory of awareness campaigns, 5K walks, donor education seminars, and free screening clinics.
* **Verified Resources:** Curated links to authoritative medical resources including the National Kidney Foundation, NIH/NIDDK, and anatomy portals.
* **Responsive Modern UI:** Accessible, mobile-friendly design with clear navigation, interactive search, and contact forms.
