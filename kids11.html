what are kidneys?


,

Kidneys are two bean-shaped organs located in the back, one on each side of the spine, that filter blood and produce urine to remove waste.
They also help regulate blood pressure, control red blood cell production, and balance electrolytes and fluids in the body.

Here's a more detailed look:


Location:


Situated in the back, just below the rib cage, on either side of the spine.

Function:


Filtering: Remove waste, excess water, and other substances from the blood, forming urine.
Fluid and Electrolyte Balance: Help maintain the right balance of water, salts, and minerals in the blood.
Hormone Production: Produce hormones like erythropoietin (stimulates red blood cell production) and renin
(helps regulate blood pressure).

Structure:

  • Cortex: The outer layer of the kidney
    • .
  • Medulla: The inner layer of the kidney, containing renal pyramids.
  • Nephrons: Tiny functional units within the kidney that filter blood.
  • Ureters: Tubes that carry urine from the kidneys to the bladder.
