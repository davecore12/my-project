Understanding Kidney Disease

Kidney disease occurs when your kidneys are damaged and can't filter blood properly. It can be chronic (gradual) or acute (sudden).

About Kidney Health

Treatment option

Treatment depends on the stage of kidney disease. It may include medications, lifestyle changes, or dialysis in severe cases.
