Understanding Kidney Disease


,

Kidney disease affects how well your kidneys filter waste and excess fluids from your blood. It can be caused by conditions like diabetes and high blood pressure, and it often progresses over time if left untreated. Symptoms can include swelling, nausea, fatigue, and difficulty sleeping. There are different types of kidney disease, including chronic kidney disease (CKD), polycystic kidney disease, and lupus nephritis. While CKD has no cure, lifestyle changes and treatments can help slow its progression.
