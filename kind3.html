Common Treatment Options for Kidney Disease

Treatment options depend directly on the disease stage and severity. Early interventions aim to preserve existing kidney function, while advanced treatments replace lost filtering capacity:

  • Medications: Targeted prescriptions like ACE inhibitors, ARBs, and SGLT2 inhibitors control blood pressure, stabilize blood glucose, and reduce protein loss (proteinuria) to slow kidney damage.
  • Lifestyle & Nutrition: Adopting a low-sodium, kidney-friendly meal plan, staying appropriately hydrated, and exercising regularly helps control symptoms and protect vessels.
  • Dialysis: In advanced kidney failure (Stage 5), hemodialysis or peritoneal dialysis filters blood mechanically when natural kidneys are no longer able to do so effectively.
  • Kidney Transplant: For end-stage renal disease, receiving a healthy donor kidney offers the highest quality of life, requiring lifelong immunosuppressive medications to prevent organ rejection.
