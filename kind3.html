Treatment options for kidney disease depend on its stage and severity. Here are some common approaches

  • Medications: Certain drugs help control blood pressure, blood sugar, and reduce protein loss in urine to slow kidney damage.
  • Lifestyle Changes: Eating a kidney-friendly diet, staying hydrated, and exercising can help manage symptoms and prevent progression.
  • Dialysis: In advanced kidney disease, dialysis helps filter waste from the blood when the kidneys can no longer do so effectively.
  • MKidney Transplant: For end-stage kidney disease, a transplant can restore kidney function, but it requires lifelong medication to prevent rejection.
